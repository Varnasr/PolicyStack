# Data Collection Methodology

## Sources

All data in PolicyStack comes from publicly available government sources. Primary sources include:

1. **Union Budget documents** (indiabudget.gov.in) -- Budget estimates, revised estimates, and actuals for scheme allocations
2. **Demand for Grants** -- Ministry-wise detailed expenditure breakdowns
3. **Scheme dashboards** -- Real-time or periodic data from scheme-specific MIS portals (MGNREGA MIS, JJM Dashboard, SBM Dashboard, NHA Dashboard, etc.)
4. **National Family Health Survey (NFHS)** -- Health and nutrition outcome indicators
5. **UDISE+** -- Education enrolment and infrastructure data
6. **Sample Registration System (SRS)** -- Vital statistics (IMR, MMR, sex ratio)
7. **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)** -- Employment and labor force data
8. **CAG Audit Reports** -- Performance audit findings

## Data Standards

- **Currency**: All budget figures are in Indian Rupees (Crores). 1 Crore = 10 million.
- **Fiscal year**: Indian fiscal year (April-March), denoted as "2023-24" meaning April 2023 to March 2024.
- **Population**: Census 2011 figures unless otherwise noted.
- **Missing data**: Blank cells indicate data not available. Do not impute.

## Limitations

- Budget "spent" figures may be provisional and subject to revision
- Scheme dashboards have varying update frequencies
- State-level disaggregation is not available for all schemes
- NFHS data is collected at ~5 year intervals (NFHS-4: 2015-16, NFHS-5: 2019-21)
- Some scheme names and structures change across administrations

## Update Frequency

- Budget data: Updated annually after Union Budget (February)
- Scheme indicators: Updated as new data releases occur
- NFHS/Census data: Updated on survey completion
